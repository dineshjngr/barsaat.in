Privacy

Your quiet stays yours.

Monsoon Radio stores your theme, shuffle, scene, volume and analytics choices in your browser. It does not require an account for ordinary listening.

Optional analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics measures general visits and interactions. Advertising storage and ad personalisation remain disabled. Choosing essential-only leaves analytics storage denied.

Music playback

The YouTube player is requested only after you press play. YouTube may then process playback information under its own privacy terms.
