ABOUT BARSAAT

Music beside a rain-lit window.

Barsaat is a small cinematic listening room inspired by monsoon rain, Indian cities and old songs drifting through an open window.

Choose a day or night atmosphere, shuffle the radio and mix the music with rain. Playback starts only when you press play.
